## Strategy Pattern

1. Intent

   Define a family of algorithms, put each of them into a separate class, and make their objects interchangeable.

2. The Context (Problem)

   You are building a Travel Booking App. Your app needs to calculate the cost of traveling from the Airport to the City Center. Currently, you support 3 types of transport:
   - Bus: Flat rate $5.

   - Taxi: $30.

   - Bicycle: $10.
